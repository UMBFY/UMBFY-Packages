# Tutorial: Protect Content Before Deletion

1. Open the content or media item you plan to remove.
2. Open **Where Used** and review inbound references.
3. Open **Impact** if you need broader dependency context.
4. Replace or remove references where appropriate.
5. Perform the delete/recycle operation.
6. Review the Safe Delete result and follow the configured policy.

Do not use this tutorial as a substitute for your organization's content-governance process.
